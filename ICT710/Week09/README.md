# Week 09

## Week Overview

Week 09 explores prototyping as a progression from rough sketches to polished interactive experiences.

## Quick Recap From Previous Week

Week 08 translated user needs into requirements, stories, and prioritized scope.

## Learning Objectives And Outcomes

Students should be able to explain why different fidelity levels exist, when to use them, and how feedback shapes iteration.

## What To Do This Week

Read `demo-plan.md`, compare the paper, wireframe, low-fi, and high-fi examples, and review the storyboard and Wizard of Oz demos.

## Key Deliverable Or Takeaway

A practical understanding that prototypes answer different questions at different stages.

## Looking Ahead To Next Week

Next week shifts from individual screens to full design systems and design languages.

# Week 10

## Week Overview

Week 10 introduces design languages and design systems through a dedicated design system studio project.

## Quick Recap From Previous Week

Week 09 focused on prototyping, iteration, and fidelity levels.

## Learning Objectives And Outcomes

Students should be able to define design tokens, build consistent foundations, and create reusable UI components within a shared visual language.

## What To Do This Week

Read `demo-plan.md`, explore the foundations and components pages, and observe how typography, color, layout, and motion work together as a system.

## Key Deliverable Or Takeaway

A clear understanding that systems scale design more effectively than isolated one-off decisions.

## Looking Ahead To Next Week

Next week focuses on design evaluation through inspection, analytics, and structured review.

# Discovery Phase - Problems Identified

## User Pain Points

1. Visual chaos from clashing colors and weak hierarchy.
2. Cognitive overload from showing too many fields at once.
3. Poor labels such as `dr` and `why`.
4. No validation for date and time entry.
5. Inconsistent input widths and spacing.
6. Aggressive CTA language that feels untrustworthy.
7. No guidance for formats or optional fields.
8. No feedback after submission.
9. Serious accessibility issues, including poor contrast.
10. No indication of progress or effort required.

## Key Insight

Users are likely to abandon the flow quickly because the form feels overwhelming and unsafe. The
simulated completion rate is only around 15%.

# Define Phase - Problem Statement

## Core Problem

Users abandon the booking process because the form is overwhelming, confusing, and lacks trust signals.

## Target Users

- Patients aged 18 to 70
- Mixed levels of digital confidence
- Mostly mobile users
- Often booking in stressful or time-sensitive situations

## Success Criteria

1. Increase completion rate from 15% to 70% or higher.
2. Keep time to complete at 3 minutes or less.
3. Limit the flow to a maximum of 3 steps.
4. Reduce date/time errors below 5%.
5. Reach an "easy to use" rating above 80%.

## Constraints

- Must collect name, email, phone, doctor, date, and time.
- Insurance and reason for visit can remain optional.
- Must work well on mobile.
- Must feel professional, private, and trustworthy.

## Design Requirements

### Must Have

- Progressive disclosure or strong sectioning
- Clear visual hierarchy
- Mobile-first design
- Helpful validation and confirmation
- Professional appearance

### Should Have

- Progress indicator
- Save-for-later concept
- Calendar picker for dates
- Time-slot selector instead of free text

### Could Have

- Remember previous bookings
- Prefill insurance info
- Alternative conversational flow later

# Week 04

## Week Overview

Week 04 applies the design process through the Double Diamond framework and the first working doctor booking app version.

## Quick Recap From Previous Week

Week 03 explored UX foundations including cognitive, social, and emotional factors.

## Learning Objectives And Outcomes

Students should be able to connect discovery, definition, development, and delivery to a working product and compare alternative interface directions.

## What To Do This Week

Read `demo-plan.md`, inspect the different booking versions, and follow how the project moves from poor design to improved alternatives.

## Key Deliverable Or Takeaway

An understanding that good interfaces emerge through iteration, not a single draft.

## Looking Ahead To Next Week

Next week focuses on data gathering techniques for design improvement.

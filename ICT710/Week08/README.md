# Week 08

## Week Overview

Week 08 focuses on requirements engineering for interaction design.

## Quick Recap From Previous Week

Week 07 used personas and user journeys to guide more targeted design choices.

## Learning Objectives And Outcomes

Students should be able to distinguish functional and non-functional requirements, write user stories, and trace requirements back to user needs.

## What To Do This Week

Read `demo-plan.md`, explore the requirements hub, and connect the dashboard views to personas, stories, use cases, and prioritization.

## Key Deliverable Or Takeaway

A structured way to move from user insight to documented design and development decisions.

## Looking Ahead To Next Week

Next week focuses on prototyping from low fidelity to high fidelity.

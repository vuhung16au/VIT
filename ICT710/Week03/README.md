# Week 03

## Week Overview

Week 03 explores UX foundations through cognitive, social, and emotional design principles.

## Quick Recap From Previous Week

Week 02 focused on problem space, conceptualization, and alternative interaction types.

## Learning Objectives And Outcomes

Students should be able to recognize how attention, memory, learning, trust, and emotion affect interface design and user behavior.

## What To Do This Week

Read `demo-plan.md`, review the Week 03 project routes, and connect each demo to a UX concept such as attention, memory, learning, trust, or testing.

## Key Deliverable Or Takeaway

A practical understanding of how UX goes beyond layout and includes thinking, feeling, and social context.

## Looking Ahead To Next Week

Next week applies design process thinking through the Double Diamond framework.

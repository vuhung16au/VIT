# Week 12

## Week Overview

Week 12 focuses on moderated usability testing as the most direct way to understand how real users experience an interface.

## Quick Recap From Previous Week

Week 11 evaluated interfaces using expert inspection and analytics methods.

## Learning Objectives And Outcomes

Students should be able to plan tests, recruit participants, moderate sessions, collect evidence, analyze findings, and present actionable recommendations.

## What To Do This Week

Read `demo-plan.md`, explore the usability lab simulator, and follow the workflow from planning through reporting.

## Key Deliverable Or Takeaway

A full view of how usability testing turns observations into concrete design improvements.

## Looking Ahead To Next Week

This is the final week of the current sequence and brings together the full IxD workflow from setup to evaluation.
